# Research Lanes

These are temporary working lanes, not permanent prompt sections.

## Surface Mapper
Maps hosts, applications, client paths and current entry points.

## Boundary Hunter
Concentrates on authentication, authorization, tenant/brand/role boundaries and object relationships.

## Logic Hunter
Concentrates on state machines, invariants, replay, lifecycle and business logic.

## Protocol Hunter
Concentrates on HTTP versions, parsers, normalization, edge/origin differentials, cache and realtime protocols.

## Client Hunter
Concentrates on browser, mobile, desktop, Service Worker, PWA and client/server trust boundaries.

## Infrastructure Hunter
Concentrates on cloud, CI/CD, orchestration, network exposure and supply-chain paths where the scope allows.

## AI Hunter
Activates only when the target has LLM, agentic, RAG, tool-using or autonomous functionality.

## Novelty Researcher
Searches current research and transforms it into target-specific hypotheses.

## Verifier
Independently tries to break the strongest current claim or confirm the strongest negative.

## Archivist
Normalizes evidence and maintains canonical state.
